PERMISSION CENTER

Android does not let JARVIS grant permissions itself. Microphone and Contacts prompts appear when requested; Call permission is requested after you confirm a call. Accessibility must be enabled manually in Android Settings. You can skip any permission, but its feature may not work.

JARVIS requests permissions only for features you use. Android or other apps may still block an action.

APP INSTALLER ASSISTANT

Say “Install app WhatsApp” or enter an app name to open its Google Play search. For APKs, enter an HTTPS link to open it in your browser. JARVIS will not silently install apps; verify the source and approve Android's installer prompt yourself.

CONTACTS & CALLING

Give JARVIS permission to search your saved contacts, then say “Call Dad”, “Call Rahul”, or “Dad ko call karo”. JARVIS will ask you to confirm before placing a call. The call request includes Android's speakerphone-start option, but the dialer/device may override it. Contact names and numbers are read locally; JARVIS does not upload your contacts to Gemini.

Voice flow: “Jarvis, call Mom” → verify the name/number → say “confirm call” or “cancel call”. Calls require Call permission and may incur carrier charges.

DAILY UTILITIES

Use voice or buttons for everyday actions. Alarms and reminders open the relevant Android app so you can review and save them.

LANGUAGE

Speech recognition availability depends on your Android speech service and selected language. Choose हिन्दी, then try Hindi or Hinglish commands like “कैमरा खोलो”, “घर जाओ”, “वापस जाओ”, “बैटरी कितनी है”, or “YouTube kholo”. For wake mode, say “Jarvis” or “जार्विस” before your command.

ACCESSIBILITY DEVICE CONTROL

Optional powerful Android permission. Enable JARVIS under Android Settings → Accessibility. Commands: “go home”, “go back”, “recent apps”, “open notifications”, “quick settings”, “scroll down”, “scroll up”, “tap Settings”, or “lock device”. This permission can inspect visible UI text to find the item you ask it to tap. Never enable accessibility for an app you do not trust.

JARVIS cannot unlock the device by collecting or typing your PIN/password, and it cannot bypass Android's lock screen. Use your phone's normal credential or biometrics.

SOCIAL APP CONTROL

Try: “open Instagram”, “Instagram search sukantcodes”, “WhatsApp search Rahul”, “type Hello, I’ll be there soon”, then “send message”. Search results must be checked so the correct account/chat is selected. “Share photo on WhatsApp”, “share video on WhatsApp”, “share photo on Instagram”, or “share video on Instagram” opens Android's picker; choose the exact media and then choose the destination. Sending/posting and selecting the recipient still needs review in the target app. Accessibility control must be enabled for in-app search, typing, and send actions; app layouts vary.

GENERAL APP CONTROL

Try “open app Calculator” or “Calculator kholo” to launch an installed app by name. With Accessibility enabled, try “tap Search”, “type your text”, “scroll down”, “go back”, or “send message” where supported. JARVIS uses visible interface controls and does not gain unrestricted access to private app data. Android and individual apps can block some actions; verify the screen before sending messages or sharing media.

GEMINI AI AGENT

Gemini can interpret natural-language requests and map them to supported Android actions. The key is entered at runtime and is not committed to GitHub. This prototype stores it in this app’s private preferences, but keys inside mobile apps can still be extracted; use a secure backend before public distribution. JARVIS still needs Android permissions, and protected app screens may block actions.
